# Content review: batch 18 (years with an Islamic-empire clue)

**3 new years**, 17 clues, each with a clue about the Islamic conquests or the empires that followed: 751 (the Abbasids and the Battle of Talas), 1526 (the Mughals at Panipat and the Ottomans at Mohács) and 1571 (the Ottomans at Lepanto and Famagusta). The other categories are well-known events from anywhere in the world. Where a category has several clues, the first is the main one and the rest are alternatives. Every clue is AI-drafted and **unverified** (`"verified": false`), so the game never shows it until you check it. A year goes live when every category has a verified clue.

**Why only three years.** Many of the best Islamic-empire years do not have a complete set of well-known events on their year pages, so I left them out:

- 711 (Tariq ibn Ziyad and Guadalete), 750 (the Abbasid Revolution) and 762 (the founding of Baghdad) have no Technology event, and 691 (the Dome of the Rock) has none either.
- 1055 (the Seljuks take Baghdad), 1071 (Manzikert), 1171 (Saladin ends the Fatimids), 1187 (Hattin) and 1250 (the Mamluks take power) are each missing one category (Technology, Society or Arts), or the missing one is only a very obscure item.
- 1291 (the Mamluks take Acre) has only obscure Society, Technology and Arts items, and 1517 (the Ottomans conquer the Mamluks) has no dated work of art and only a weak Technology item (the death of Luca Pacioli). Both had Politics clues I could use.

If you would like one of these, say which category you can accept being thin, or give me an event for the missing category, and I will add the year.

**Date issues:**

- 751 Talas: July is the usual date, but one source says June. The papermaking story is disputed, and the clue says "is said to". The Great Buddha at Tōdai-ji: the year page says 751, but the eye-opening ceremony was in 752.
- 1526: Mohács 29 August and Panipat 21 April are firm. The Moriscos' deadline was 26 January 1526 (year page). Tyndale's Testament reached England in the spring and was condemned in October.
- 1571: Lepanto 7 October is firm. Kepler (27 December) and Caravaggio (29 September) are the dates the year page gives, and Caravaggio's birth is sometimes questioned.

**From my own knowledge, not looked up (check first):** the Pepin and Pope Zachary details; the Talas context; Kim Daeseong dying before Bulguksa was finished; the Great Buddha's ceremony in 752; Panipat's war elephants and the Mughals ruling until the nineteenth century; the length of Mohács and the Ottoman hold on Hungary; the Moriscos' background; Tyndale's burning and execution; the Cervantes wound at Lepanto; Ivan's flight from Moscow; Hugh Price and Jesus College; Kepler's patrons; Caravaggio's later life; Gresham and the Royal Exchange.

**Weakest year: 751.** Its Society, Technology and Arts clues are from Korea, Central Asia and Japan and are probably too obscure for most players. The Pepin clue is the best known. You may prefer to drop 751 and keep 1526 and 1571.
