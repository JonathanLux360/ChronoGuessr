**Things to check (from my own knowledge, not looked up):**

- 1919 Ho Chi Minh: he had been living in Paris for a couple of years by then, so the clue says he petitions the Allied leaders at Versailles, not that he travels to Paris. The petition was presented in June 1919 under the name Nguyen Ai Quoc, and he never met Wilson.
- 1920 Warsaw: the battle of 12 to 25 August and the name "Miracle on the Vistula".
- 1921 Švejk: the novel was published in instalments in 1921 to 1923 and left unfinished at Hašek's death in January 1923. The clue is about the start of publication, in 1921.
- 1955 Family of Man: it opened at the Museum of Modern Art on 24 January 1955, with more than five hundred photographs from dozens of countries.
- 1974 Cyprus: the coup was on 15 July and the Turkish invasion on 20 July. The clue says "days later".
