# Content review: batch 12 (the 1600s)

**5 new years** from the 1600s: 1623, 1643, 1656, 1665 and 1698. Technology and Arts & Culture were found first, then Politics and Society to match. Where a category has two clues, the first is the main clue and the second is an alternative (the game draws one at random). Every clue is AI-drafted and **unverified** (`"verified": false`), so the game never shows it until you check it. A year goes live when every category has a verified clue. For each clue, open the source and check:

1. The event happened **in that year** (not across a span).
2. The text and the context line are accurate and neutral.
3. The clue does not give the year away on its own.
4. The source link works and supports the date.

The dates come from the Wikipedia year pages. I read the year pages and opened the articles for the Technology and Arts events I could. Where an article agreed with the year page it is noted below.

**Checked against the article:** Calderón's first play (29 June 1623, Royal Alcázar); the Taj Mahal first ceremony (6 February 1643); Torricelli's experiment ("in 1643", year only); the pendulum clock (invented 25 December 1656, patented 1657); Lower's transfusion (February 1665); Molière's Dom Juan (15 February 1665); Savery's patent (1698; the year page gives both 2 July and 25 July, so the day is unclear, and the clue gives no day).
