# Context lines review

The context line is shown on the result screen, after the guess, under each clue the player saw. I rewrote **582** of them. Most used to say only the month ("It happened in June."). Now each adds something more: the significance of the event, the reception of a work, where it happened or was performed, or what came next. The month is kept where it helps.

**These new facts are mine, written from memory, and have not been checked against the sources.** The `verified` flag only covers the date of the clue, so I did not change it. Of the clues below, 367 are verified and live in the game, and 215 are still unverified drafts. Please read the new lines, and tell me about any that are wrong or too strong, so I can correct them.

Things worth a second look: numbers (audiences, death tolls, page counts, speeds), "first" and "only" claims, and anything about how a work was received.
