# Content review — batch 9 (458 BCE to 1469)

**20 new years** from before 1500: 75 clues ready to check, plus alternatives for five categories that have no exact-year clue yet. Each year has one clue in every category once you pick an option where one is offered. Every clue is AI-drafted and **unverified** (`"verified": false`), so the game never shows it until you check it. For each clue, open the source and check:

1. The event happened **in that year** (not across a span).
2. The text and the context line are accurate and neutral.
3. The clue does not give the year away on its own.
4. The source link works and supports the date.

Tick a year only when all its clues pass. A year goes live when every category has a verified clue.

**Storage.** CE years are stored as plain numbers (132 is 132). BCE years use astronomical numbering, one minus the BCE number (458 BCE is stored as -457). The game's year range and scoring still need to change to allow this.

**Options tables.** Where a year has no clue with an exact year in some category, it has an options table underneath. Pick one (tell me the letter) and it goes into the year's table. The "How exact" column says how far off each option is. Until you pick, that year cannot go live.

The Source column links to the article for each event, and I have not opened those. The limit of two European clues per year is not applied here, but no year has all four clues from one region. Technology clues favour the first notable use of a technology (in a battle, a siege or an outbreak).
