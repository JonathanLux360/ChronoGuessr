# Content review: batch 11 (Crusades and Mongol invasions)

**3 new years** from the era of the Crusades and the Mongol invasions: 1258, 1279 and 1291. Technology and Arts & Culture were found first, then Politics and Society to match. Every clue is AI-drafted and **unverified** (`"verified": false`), so the game never shows it until you check it. A year goes live when every category has a verified clue. For each clue, open the source and check:

1. The event happened **in that year** (not across a span).
2. The text and the context line are accurate and neutral.
3. The clue does not give the year away on its own.
4. The source link works and supports the date.

**Details from memory, not from the year pages. Please check these first:**

- 1258: Simon de Montfort among the barons; Venice winning the Battle of Acre and the war lasting years; Salisbury being built quickly in one style and the spire being added later.
- 1279: Traidenis as the Lithuanian leader, and the Livonian master being lost; the Mortmain law's reason (Church land paying no dues); Faraj ben Salim working for Charles of Anjou, and the Al-Hawi helping to pass on Arab medicine. The Faraj article is inconsistent about which book was translated in 1279.
- 1291: Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil; the siege lasting about six weeks and the other towns falling within months; the Vivaldi brothers' two galleys and never returning; Kunming Lake being in the Summer Palace grounds; Kameyama's palace becoming the temple and the later Zen ranking.

**Weak points:**

- 1258 Society: the Battle of Acre is really a naval battle in a trade war. It is filed under Society as a trade rivalry. The year page also lists smaller Society items (a Maldives island converting to Islam, forced loans in Genoa) that I could not check.
- 1258 Technology: the siege ran from the end of January into February, and its start day is given as the 29th or 30th. The Mongols entered the city on 15 February.
- 1279 Technology: the battle's technology is modest. The Song fleet was chained together and coated in mud, and no cannons were used.
- 1279 Arts & Culture: a medical translation is a stretch for the category. It is the weakest clue in the batch.
- 1291 Technology: the year page says Guo "constructs" the reservoir, but the Kunming Lake article says he developed an existing lake. The clue follows the article.
- 1291 Society: the Vivaldi voyage is a spring event. Some historians doubt the story.

**Checked and left out.** 963 has Politics and Society (Nikephoros II crowned, Otto I deposes John XII) but no exact-year Technology or Arts & Culture event on its page. 1099, 1187 and 1204 each lack an exact-year Technology event. 1241, 1260 and 1271 lack a firm Technology or Arts & Culture clue (1260's Chartres re-consecration is firm for the year, but its Technology is empty). 1219 has no Arts & Culture clue, and 1281 has no Technology clue.
