# Content review: batch 14 (gaps in 1900 to 2019, and the 1700s)

**13 years**, 78 clues: the nine missing years between 1900 and 2019 (1902, 1904, 1907, 1909, 2000, 2002, 2006, 2015 and 2017) and four from the 1700s (1721, 1745, 1776 and 1789). Technology includes science milestones, and Arts & Culture includes famous works, songs and the deaths of famous writers. Where a category has several clues, the first is the main clue and the rest are alternatives (the game draws one at random). Every clue is AI-drafted and **unverified** (`"verified": false`), so the game never shows it until you check it. A year goes live when every category has a verified clue. For each clue, open the source and check:

1. The event happened **in that year** (not across a span).
2. The text and the context line are accurate and neutral.
3. The clue does not give the year away on its own.
4. The source link works and supports the date.

**Trimmed for obscurity.** 29 clues that most players could not place were dropped at your request, and seven replacements were drafted (listed under "New clues" below). Kept on purpose, although flagged: Rachmaninoff's Third Piano Concerto (1909), the Anglo-Russian Entente (1907) and Itō Hirobumi's assassination (1909). The 1909 Society clue is now the founding of the NAACP.

**What was checked.** The dates come from the Wikipedia year pages and the linked articles I could open. For 1902, 1904, 1907 and 1909 most Politics and Society items came only from a page summary, so those are the least checked. The seven replacements are from my own knowledge and were not looked up this session, so check them first.
