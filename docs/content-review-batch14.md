# Content review: batch 14 (gaps in 1900 to 2019, and the 1700s)

**13 new years**, 101 clues: the nine missing years between 1900 and 2019 (1902, 1904, 1907, 1909, 2000, 2002, 2006, 2015 and 2017) and four from the 1700s (1721, 1745, 1776 and 1789). Technology includes science milestones and Arts & Culture includes famous works, songs and the deaths of famous writers. Where a category has several clues, the first is the main clue and the rest are alternatives (the game draws one at random). Every clue is AI-drafted and **unverified** (`"verified": false`), so the game never shows it until you check it. A year goes live when every category has a verified clue. For each clue, open the source and check:

1. The event happened **in that year** (not across a span).
2. The text and the context line are accurate and neutral.
3. The clue does not give the year away on its own.
4. The source link works and supports the date.
